The problem

Real reach, all of it rented

On air everywhere

NFL on Seven with Ben Graham. Racing mornings on SEN Track. The Trade Feed on AFL Trade Radio. NBL Overtime with ESPN. The Armchair Experts podcasts.

None of it owned

Every listener belongs to a network’s platform — Seven’s, SEN’s, the AFL’s. If a contract changes, the audience stays with the station, not with Cam.

Dark at the wrong time

The podcast went quiet at the AFL Grand Final — right as the spring carnival, the NFL season and NBL27 hit their peak, all codes Cam works in daily.

Cam is a year-round broadcaster with a seasonal brand. The gap between those two things is the opportunity.
