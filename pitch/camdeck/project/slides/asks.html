  • A cadence he can keep year-round — even twenty minutes a week between seasons

What the platform already does

  • Pulls every episode and clip in automatically — no uploading, no admin
  • Keeps 15 codes of live data fresh — the daily-visit reason runs itself

  • Tracks what the audience actually clicks, so decisions use data

Nothing here competes with Seven, SEN, the AFL or ESPN — it makes their Cam more valuable while building the asset that is his.
