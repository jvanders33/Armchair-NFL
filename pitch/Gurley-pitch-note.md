## THE SHOW

**Running it Back.** Weekly through the NFL season. You bring the player's eye — what actually happens on that field, what a locker room sounds like in December, who's about to get found out. I bring the audience that doesn't know any of it yet and desperately wants to.

Australia is the most under-served football market in the world right now. In five weeks the NFL plays its first ever game here — 100,000 people at the MCG — and there is no established local show waiting to catch that audience. That's the gap, and it opens on 11 September. You'd be the biggest name in it from day one.

The best part: **Sunday football in America is Monday daytime in Australia.** Every week, the biggest day in your sport lands in the middle of our working week, and nobody is programming for it. We would be.

---

## THE SHAPE OF IT

This isn't a one-season podcast. It's a two-year run with a marquee in the middle:

**Season 1 — NFL 2026/27.** Weekly show. You in LA, me in Melbourne. We launch straight into the MCG game, when the whole country is watching football for the first time.

**Super Bowl LXI — February 2027.** SoFi Stadium, your city. **Four shows on the ground across the week, both of us there.** The biggest week in the sport, with a season of the show already behind us.

**Season 2 — NFL 2027/28.** Weekly again, into an audience that's had a full year to build, with a second Super Bowl run to close it out.

Two full seasons, and the biggest week in football comes to you.

---

## THE DEAL — AND WHY IT'S BUILT THIS WAY

I want you to make as much money out of this as it can possibly make. That's not me being generous, it's me being smart: if this thing grows, I want you as motivated as I am to grow it.

So it's two parts:

**1. A fixed fee per episode — [$X].** Guaranteed. You get paid for every show you turn up to, regardless of how the business is going. Your time has a price and this respects it.

**2. Equity — [X]% of the show.** You own a piece of the actual business. Ad revenue, sponsorship, the back catalogue, whatever it becomes. If it goes big, you don't get a bonus — you get your share.

The fee is your floor. The equity is your ceiling, and there's no cap on it.

Look at what's happened in the last few years: McAfee, the Kelces, the whole wave of it. Every one of those started as a small show with a big name attached, and the people who took the ownership early are the ones who made the real money. That window is closed in America. It's wide open here.

## WHO'S BEHIND IT

**iHeart America and iHeart Australia** — a genuine co-production across both markets. They're meeting this week to lock in how it works. That means production, distribution and commercial are carried by them, not by us.

What you'd actually be committing to: **roughly two hours a week in season, remote from LA.** Plus Super Bowl week, which is on your doorstep anyway.

## WHAT I NEED FROM YOU

Just whether the shape of it feels right. If it does, iHeart puts real numbers against the fee and the equity and we paper it properly.

I've spent seven years doing this on Australian television and I've got the audience and the platform ready to go. What I don't have is you. That's the piece that makes it the show instead of a show.

Have a think, and let's talk this week.
